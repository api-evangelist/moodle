Moodle announced an enhanced Certified Integrations program with three tiers -- Certified Integration, Certified Integration Plus, and Certified Integration Premium -- that independently vets third-party tools for quality, security, and compatibility. New certified integrations include EduGears AI, Kortext IQ, Nudgis by Ubicast, and Quilgo, alongside established partners like BigBlueButton, ReadSpeaker, and SMOWL.
